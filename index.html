01 · FROM MY HEART

Things I should have
said earlier...

♡A letter for you♡

My dearest Yogyata,

I know I have done many, many things wrong. I behaved rudely, made you cry, disappointed you, let you down, shouted at you, blamed you and said things I should never have said.

Not a single person deserves to be treated that way. Not even you, and certainly not by someone who says he loves you.

I am ashamed of the way I reacted, the words I used in anger and the disrespect I showed you. Even if I didn't mean those words, I know that doesn't undo the hurt they caused.

I kept saying that I loved you, but I didn't always take care of your emotions. Instead of understanding you, I blamed you. I kept bringing up unnecessary things and making you sad.

You deserved patience, kindness, understanding and respect. I am truly sorry that I failed to give you those things.

I know forgiveness isn't something I can demand from you. Maybe some of the hurt will take a long time to heal, and I understand that.

But I want you to know that I don't want to lose you. I understand that you have fears, especially the fear that if you let yourself love me completely, I might eventually leave. I don't want to dismiss those fears or ask you to ignore them.

I want to become someone whose actions make you feel safe, respected and valued. I want to talk to you, adore you, listen to you, understand your emotions and make you smile. I want to work on myself, not just make promises.

From the very first day I saw you until today, you have been someone incredibly special to me. I have imagined a future with you — marriage, a sweet little family, building our dreams together and working hard so we can live a peaceful and happy life.

I still dream of those things. But more than anything, I want our connection to be a place where both of us feel respected, heard and safe.

I don't want endless fights. I don't want to keep hurting you. I want to learn how to love you better.

You don't have to give me an answer today. You don't have to promise me anything. I just wanted you to know how deeply sorry I am, how important you are to me, and how sincerely I want to become better.

With all my heart,
your Arpit ♡
